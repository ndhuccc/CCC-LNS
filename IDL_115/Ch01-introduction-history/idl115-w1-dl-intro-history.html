Part B · What Is Deep Learning, Anyway? | 深度學習到底是什麼

9A Model Is a Map, Not the Territory | 模型是地圖，不是地圖所指的實地

A useful model has to do two things: explain what we've already observed, and predict what we haven't seen yet.
— on model usefulness | 關於模型有沒有用

A good model doesn't have to be "true" — it has to be useful: it explains past data and predicts future data. | 好模型不必然要「為真」——它必須有用：解釋過去的觀測，並預測未來的觀測。

The map (model) is a simplified, useful representation — never the real terrain (the world) itself. | 地圖（模型）是簡化但有用的表示法——它終究不是真正的地形（真實世界）本身。

The 3-Step Machine Learning Recipe | 機器學習三步驟食譜

"Propose a modeling hypothesis, then use data to test and correct it" — echoing Karl Popper's philosophy of science. | 「先提出一個建模假設，再用資料去檢驗、修正它」——呼應 Popper 的科學哲學。

1
Hypothesis Class | 假設類別

What kind of model? Linear, tree-based, neural network... | 定義建模類型：線性、樹、神經網路……

2
Loss Function | 評分機制

A number that quantifies how bad a model is. | 用損失函數量化模型好壞。

3
Optimization | 搜尋策略

Solve an optimization problem to find the lowest-loss model. | 求解最佳化問題，找出損失最低的模型。

$$\widehat{f} := \arg\min_{f \in \mathcal{F}} S(f)$$

"Find the function $f$, out of an allowed family $\mathcal{F}$, that minimizes the score $S(f)$." | 「在一個允許的函數家族 $\mathcal{F}$ 中，找出讓評分 $S(f)$ 最小的那個函數 $f$。」

No Free Lunch: no learning algorithm beats all others on every possible problem — unless it first assumes something about the world (an inductive bias). | 沒有免費午餐定理：沒有任何學習演算法能在所有問題上都表現最好——除非它先對世界做了假設（歸納偏置）。

10Turning the World Into Vectors | 把世界變成向量

Every object is described by $d$ features → a $d$-dimensional vector $\boldsymbol{x}_i \in \mathbb{R}^d$; $n$ samples stack into a matrix $\boldsymbol{X} \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times n}$. | 每個物件用 $d$ 個特徵描述，變成一個 $d$ 維向量 $\boldsymbol{x}_i \in \mathbb{R}^d$；$n$ 個樣本排成矩陣 $\boldsymbol{X} \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times n}$。

Any object, once described by enough features, becomes just a point (a vector) sitting inside a shared numeric space. | 任何物件只要被夠多的特徵描述，就會變成一個座落在共同數值空間裡的點（向量）。

$$f_{\theta}: \mathbb{R}^{d} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{p}$$

A learning model is just a function from data-space to output-space, with learnable parameters $\theta$. | 學習模型就是一個從資料空間映射到輸出空間的函數，帶有可學習的參數 $\theta$。

11Three Flavors of Learning | 三種學習方式

Same data, three different jobs: sort it (supervised), group it (unsupervised), or learn from a reward signal (reinforcement). | 同樣的資料，三種不同任務：分類標記（監督式）、自動分組（非監督式），或從獎勵訊號中學習（強化學習）。

Framework anatomy: inputs and feedback determine the learning problem. | 框架解剖：輸入與回饋決定學習問題。

👀 Supervised | 監督式

Classification: $f_\theta: \mathbb{R}^d \to \{\ell_1,\dots,\ell_m\}$ — discrete labels. | 分類：離散標籤。
Regression: $f_\theta: \mathbb{R}^d \to \mathbb{R}$ — a continuous value. | 回歸：連續數值。

🧩 Unsupervised | 非監督式

Clustering (no labels) and dimensionality reduction $f_\theta: \mathbb{R}^d \to \mathbb{R}^p,\ p \ll d$. | 分群（無標籤）與降維。

🎮 Reinforcement | 強化學習

An agent interacts with an environment, choosing actions $a \in \mathcal{A}$ to maximize cumulative reward. Think AlphaGo. | 代理與環境互動，選擇動作以最大化累積獎勵，例如 AlphaGo。

12Classification vs. Regression | 分類 vs. 回歸

Aspect | 面向Classification | 分類Regression | 回歸
Output | 輸出Finite label set $\{\ell_1,\dots,\ell_m\}$ | 有限標籤集合Any real number $\mathbb{R}$ | 任意實數
Example | 例子Apple vs. cucumber | 蘋果 vs. 小黃瓜Predicting an apple's diameter | 預測蘋果直徑

Six classic statistical families — Bayes/Naive Bayes, LDA/QDA, Logistic Regression, KNN, Decision Trees/Random Forests, and (kernel) SVM — all predate deep learning and still matter as baselines. | 統計分類的六大經典家族——Bayes／Naive Bayes、LDA／QDA、羅吉斯迴歸、KNN、決策樹／隨機森林、（核）SVM——都早於深度學習出現，至今仍是重要的基準方法。

13Early Neural Network History (1943–1969) | 早期神經網路史

1943 · McCulloch–Pitts Neuron | 神經元

The first mathematical model of a neuron: binary inputs, threshold logic — could express AND/OR/NOT. | 首個神經元數學模型：二元輸入、閾值邏輯，能表達 AND/OR/NOT。

1949 · Hebbian Learning | Hebb 學習

"Cells that fire together, wire together." Weights adjust by correlation — an early form of learning. | 「一起激發的細胞，會連在一起。」依相關性調整權重，是學習的雛形。

1958 · Perceptron (Rosenblatt) | 感知器

A single neuron that learns from examples — a linear classifier. | 能從範例學習的單層神經元，是線性分類器。

1969 · XOR Limitation | XOR 限制

Minsky & Papert prove the Perceptron can't solve XOR — triggering the first AI winter for neural nets. | Minsky 與 Papert 證明感知器無法解 XOR，引發神經網路的第一次寒冬。

From One Neuron to a Universe of Them | 從一個神經元到一整片神經網路

From a single McCulloch–Pitts neuron (1943) to today's massive deep networks — the same basic idea, scaled up across eight decades. | 從一顆 McCulloch–Pitts 神經元（1943）到今日龐大的深度網路——同樣的基本想法，橫跨八十年不斷擴大規模。

14Modern History: From Winter to Deep Learning Boom | 現代史：從寒冬到全面爆發

1980s–2006 · AI Winter | AI 冬眠

Vanishing/exploding gradients, weak hardware, small datasets — SVMs and kernel methods dominate instead. | 梯度消失／爆炸、硬體算力不足、資料集太小——SVM 與核方法成為主流。

2006 · Deep Belief Networks (Hinton) | DBN 復興

Unsupervised layer-wise pretraining finally makes deep nets trainable again. | 無監督逐層預訓練，讓深度網路重新變得可訓練。

2012 · AlexNet | AlexNet 突破

GPUs + ReLU + big data crush the ImageNet benchmark — and deep learning explodes. | GPU＋ReLU＋大數據刷新 ImageNet 成績——深度學習全面熱潮由此點燃。

2014 · VAE & GAN | 生成模型元年

Two very different ways to generate new data: variational autoencoders and adversarial networks. | 兩種截然不同的資料生成方式：變分自編碼器與對抗生成網路。

2017–18 · Transformer, BERT, GPT | 注意力時代

Self-attention replaces recurrence — the architecture behind every modern LLM. | 自注意力取代遞迴，成為所有現代大型語言模型的基礎架構。

2020–21 · DDPM & Diffusion Models | 擴散模型崛起

DDPM makes iterative denoising practical for high-quality synthesis; 2021 results surpass GANs on ImageNet. | DDPM 讓逐步加噪、再學習反向去噪的高品質生成變得實用；2021 年的結果在 ImageNet 上超越 GAN。

2022 · ChatGPT | 對話式生成式 AI 普及

A GPT-3.5–based conversational research preview brings generative AI to the general public at unprecedented scale. | 以 GPT-3.5 系列為基礎的對話式研究預覽，讓生成式 AI 以前所未有的規模走進大眾使用情境。

This entire 77-year story — Perceptron to Transformer — is exactly the syllabus of this course, in order. | 這整整 77 年的故事——從感知器到 Transformer——正好就是這門課接下來的教學順序。

Concept | 概念題
The No Free Lunch theorem says a learning algorithm needs an inductive bias to beat random guessing on a specific problem. What does "inductive bias" mean here? | 沒有免費午餐定理指出，學習演算法需要歸納偏置才能在特定問題上勝過隨機猜測。這裡「歸納偏置」是什麼意思？
✅ Answer | 答案：
It's the set of assumptions a model makes about the world before seeing any data — e.g. "nearby points tend to have similar labels." Without some such assumption, no algorithm can generalize better than any other, on average across all possible problems. | 是模型在看到資料之前，就對世界做的一組假設——例如「鄰近的點傾向有相似的標籤」。沒有這樣的假設，任何演算法在所有可能問題的平均表現上，都不會比其他演算法更好。
3
History | 歷史題
What breakthrough in 2012 is widely credited with reigniting deep learning after decades of AI winter? | 2012 年的哪一項突破，普遍被認為是重新點燃深度學習熱潮的關鍵？
✅ Answer | 答案：
AlexNet — a deep CNN trained on GPUs with ReLU activations, which dramatically outperformed all prior methods on the ImageNet competition. | AlexNet——一個用 GPU 訓練、採用 ReLU 激活函數的深度 CNN，在 ImageNet 競賽中大幅超越所有先前方法。
