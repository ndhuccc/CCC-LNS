3關鍵一步：把「東西」變成「一個點」| From Object to Vector

這是整門課的門檻。跨過去後面一路順風，跨不過去每一章都是外星語。
This is the threshold of the whole course. Cross it and everything after is smooth sailing; fail to cross it and every chapter reads like alien language.

我們不處理「貓」，我們處理「代表貓的一串數字」 | We Don't Process "Cats" — We Process "A String of Numbers Representing a Cat"

用兩個數字描述一隻動物：$x_1$ = 體重（kg），$x_2$ = 叫聲基頻（kHz）。
Describe an animal with two numbers: $x_1$ = weight (kg), $x_2$ = call fundamental frequency (kHz).

$$x = \begin{bmatrix} 4.6 \\ 1.12 \end{bmatrix}$$

這叫特徵向量 feature vector——名字唬人，其實就是「一張只填了兩格的資料卡」。
This is called a feature vector — an intimidating name for what is really just "a data card with only two boxes filled in."

每隻動物變成一張卡片後就能畫在一張紙上（特徵空間 feature space）。於是「這是貓還是狗？」變成了 「這個點掉在紙的哪一區？」
Once every animal becomes a card, you can plot it on a sheet of paper (feature space). "Is this a cat or a dog?" becomes "Which region of the paper does this point fall in?"

4那條界線怎麼畫？| Drawing the Boundary

最直覺的畫法：畫在兩群點的正中間——線上每一點到兩類平均一樣遠。
The most intuitive approach: draw it right down the middle between the two clusters — every point on the line is equally far from both class means.

$$g(x) = w^{\top}x + b = 0, \qquad w = m_1 - m_2, \qquad b = -\,w^{\top}\frac{m_1+m_2}{2}$$

$w^{\top}x$ 別怕：它只是「對應位置相乘再相加」。就像結帳——$w$ 是單價，$x$ 是數量，$w^{\top}x$ 是總金額。
Don't be scared of $w^{\top}x$: it's just "multiply matching positions, then add them up." Like a checkout receipt — $w$ is unit price, $x$ is quantity, $w^{\top}x$ is the total.

1
取兩類平均 | Average the Two Classes

貓 $\omega_1$：$m_1 = (4.5,\ 1.10)$　　狗 $\omega_2$：$m_2 = (12.0,\ 0.45)$

2
算方向 $w$ | Compute Direction $w$

$w = m_1 - m_2 = (4.5-12.0,\ \ 1.10-0.45) = (-7.5,\ \ 0.65)$

3
算截距 $b$ | Compute Intercept $b$

中點 $=(8.25,\ 0.775)$，故 $b = -[(-7.5)(8.25)+(0.65)(0.775)] = -[-61.875+0.50375] = 61.37$

4
得到界線 | Get the Boundary

$g(x) = -7.5\,x_1 + 0.65\,x_2 + 61.37 = 0$

5實際判一隻新動物 | A Worked Example

來了一隻沒見過的動物：$x = (7.0,\ 0.95)$。牠是貓還是狗？
A never-before-seen animal shows up: $x=(7.0, 0.95)$. Is it a cat or a dog?

$$g(x) = (-7.5)(7.0) + (0.65)(0.95) + 61.37 = -52.5 + 0.6175 + 61.37 = +9.49$$

$g(x) > 0$

判給 $\omega_1$（貓）。本例 $+9.49$，離界線很遠，把握很大。
Classify as $\omega_1$ (cat). In this example, $+9.49$ — far from the boundary, high confidence.

$g(x) < 0$

判給 $\omega_2$（狗）。
Classify as $\omega_2$ (dog).

$g(x) = 0$

剛好站在界線上——恭喜，這隻動物讓機器人生迷惘。
Sitting exactly on the boundary — congratulations, this animal has left the machine existentially confused.

「離界線多遠 = 有多少把握」這個直覺，會一路撐到 §3 的 SVM：既然離越遠越安心，那就把界線畫在讓最近的點離得最遠的位置。
The intuition "distance from the boundary = confidence" carries all the way to SVM in §3: since farther is safer, why not draw the boundary where the nearest points are as far as possible?

📈 圖 2：特徵空間、決策邊界與新樣本（依上述數值精算繪製） | Fig. 2: Feature space, decision boundary, and the new sample (plotted from the exact numbers above)
注意紅線幾乎垂直——因為 $w=(-7.5,\ 0.65)$ 裡體重那一項權重大得多。等於機器自己說：「叫聲這個特徵，參考價值不高。」

Notice the red line is nearly vertical — because in $w=(-7.5, 0.65)$ the weight on body weight is far larger. The machine is effectively saying: "call frequency isn't very informative." The magnitude of $w$ = the machine telling you which feature matters. This is the seed of the entire §5 feature-selection chapter.

7如果沒有人告訴你答案？| When There Is No Teacher

監督式：分類 Classification

資料帶著標籤（有老師改考卷）。目標：學一條分界，用來判新資料。
Data comes with labels (a teacher grades the exam). Goal: learn a boundary to classify new data.

對應：§2 貝氏決策 · §3 線性 · §4 非線性 · §8 樣板匹配

非監督式：分群 Clustering

資料沒有標籤（老闆說「幫我看看客人分成幾種」）。目標：自己找出誰跟誰是一夥的。
Data has no labels (the boss says "figure out how many types of customers we have"). Goal: figure out on your own who belongs with whom.

對應：§10 循序分群 · §11 階層式／最佳化／效度評估

分類是「有人告訴你有哪些抽屜，你把東西放對抽屜」；分群是「沒有抽屜，你要自己決定該做幾個抽屜、每個裝什麼」。
Classification is "someone tells you what drawers exist, and you put things in the right one"; clustering is "there are no drawers — you decide how many to make and what goes in each."
—— 這就是為什麼 §11 要花整章討論「我分出來的群到底好不好」 | This is why §11 spends a whole chapter on "is my clustering actually any good"

8那……跟深度學習有什麼關係？| Where Does Deep Learning Fit?

深度學習把「表示」和「決策」綁在一起讓機器自己學；這門課把兩步拆開來，讓你看清每一步的原理、假設與代價。
Deep learning bundles "representation" and "decision" together and lets the machine learn both; this course pulls the two steps apart so you can see the principle, assumptions, and cost of each.
理由一：資料少的時候，深度學習學不出好特徵 | Reason 1: With little data, deep learning can't learn good features▼

這時你必須自己設計特徵。§5–§7 就是你的救命工具箱。
In that case you must design features yourself — §5–§7 is your survival toolbox.

理由二：深度網路的最後一層，幾乎都是線性分類器 | Reason 2: The last layer of a deep network is almost always a linear classifier▼

你今天學的 $g(x)=w^{\top}x+b$ 就是那一層。差別只在 $x$ 是人設計的、還是網路自己算出來的。
The $g(x)=w^{\top}x+b$ you learned today is exactly that layer — the only difference is whether $x$ is hand-designed or learned by the network.

理由三：出錯時你要知道錯在哪一步 | Reason 3: When things break, you need to know which step failed▼

是特徵沒挑好，還是界線畫錯了？沒拆開過，你永遠只能說「模型不 work」，然後把學習率再調小一點，祈禱。
Was it a bad feature, or a bad boundary? Without separating the steps, all you can ever say is "the model doesn't work," lower the learning rate, and pray.

這門課不是深度學習的競爭對手，它是深度學習的除錯手冊。
This course isn't a competitor to deep learning — it's deep learning's debugging manual.

知識檢定 | Knowledge Check

1
數值計算 | Numerical Calculation
若新樣本 $x = (9.0,\ 0.80)$，請算出 $g(x) = -7.5x_1 + 0.65x_2 + 61.37$ 並判斷類別。
Given a new sample $x=(9.0, 0.80)$, compute $g(x)=-7.5x_1+0.65x_2+61.37$ and determine its class.
✅ 正確答案：
$g = -7.5(9.0) + 0.65(0.80) + 61.37 = -67.5 + 0.52 + 61.37 = -5.61 < 0$，判給 $\omega_2$（狗）。 注意 $|{-5.61}|$ 比先前的 $9.49$ 小，代表它離界線比較近、把握比較低——這正是「邊界附近的樣本最難判」的具體展現。
$g=-7.5(9.0)+0.65(0.80)+61.37=-67.5+0.52+61.37=-5.61<0$, so classify as $\omega_2$ (dog). Note $|{-5.61}|$ is smaller than the earlier $9.49$ — closer to the boundary, lower confidence, exactly the "samples near the boundary are hardest" phenomenon in action.
2
概念理解 | Conceptual Understanding
為什麼權重 $w$ 中「叫聲頻率」只有 $0.65$，而「體重」有 $-7.5$？這告訴我們什麼？
Why is the weight for "call frequency" only $0.65$ while "weight" is $-7.5$? What does this tell us?
✅ 正確答案：
因為兩類在「體重」上的平均差了 $7.5$ kg，在「叫聲」上只差 $0.65$ kHz。差距大的特徵，權重大，對決策的貢獻也大。 這同時暴露一個陷阱：權重也受單位影響——若把體重改用公克，$w_1$ 會變成 $-7500$。所以實務上必須先做特徵正規化（§5 會談）。
Because the two classes' means differ by $7.5$ kg in weight but only $0.65$ kHz in call frequency. A feature with a bigger gap between classes gets a bigger weight and contributes more to the decision. This also exposes a trap: weights are also affected by units — switch weight to grams and $w_1$ becomes $-7500$. In practice you must normalize features first (covered in §5).
3
舉一反三 | Generalizing the Idea
「把一萬封信自動歸到垃圾／非垃圾」與「把一萬名顧客分成幾種消費型態」，分別屬於分類還是分群？
"Automatically sorting ten thousand emails into spam/not-spam" versus "grouping ten thousand customers into spending patterns" — which is classification and which is clustering?
✅ 正確答案：
前者是分類（抽屜已經定好：垃圾／非垃圾，且有標好的訓練信件）； 後者是分群（沒人事先定義有幾種顧客，連「幾群才合理」都要自己決定）。 判斷訣竅：問「有沒有人先把答案告訴我？」
The first is classification (the drawers are already fixed: spam/not-spam, with labeled training emails); the second is clustering (nobody predefined how many customer types exist — even "how many groups make sense" must be decided on your own). The rule of thumb: ask "did anyone already tell me the answer?"

總結回顧 | Summary

如果今天只帶走一段話 | If You Take Away Only One Sentence Today

圖形識別 = 把物件變成向量（表示）→ 在向量空間裡切開（決策）→ 檢查切得好不好（評估）。
有標籤時叫分類，沒標籤時叫分群。
接下來十六週，只是在把這三步的每一步講得越來越細、越來越誠實。
Pattern recognition = turn objects into vectors (representation) → cut the vector space (decision) → check whether the cut was good (evaluation). With labels it's called classification; without labels it's called clustering. The next sixteen weeks just make each of these three steps more detailed and more honest.

核心工具 | Core Tool

線性判別函數 $g(x)=w^{\top}x+b$：正負決定類別，絕對值大小決定把握。
Linear discriminant function $g(x)=w^{\top}x+b$: the sign decides the class, the magnitude decides the confidence.
