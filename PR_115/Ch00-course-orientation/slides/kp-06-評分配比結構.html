15 / 25 / 25 / 5 / 20 / 10 的權重設計
The design behind the 15/25/25/5/20/10 weighting

PR115 圖形識別 · §1 課程說明與導論 · KP-06

老師把 25% 押在哪裡，就是在說「這件事我認為值 25 分」。
配分不是隨便填的，它是課程目標的價格標籤。
Wherever the professor puts 25%, that's them saying "I think this is worth 25 points." Weighting isn't filled in arbitrarily — it's the price tag on the course's goals.

2為什麼考試佔一半？| Why Exams Weigh 50%

因為這門課教的是判斷力，而判斷力最難用專案驗收。
Because this course teaches judgment, and judgment is the hardest thing to verify through a project.

學生 A | Student A

專案效能 94%，很厲害。但他是把三個 GitHub repo 拼起來，不知道為什麼有用。
Project accuracy 94%, impressive. But he stitched together three GitHub repos and has no idea why it works.

學生 B | Student B

專案只到 78%，但他說得出：選 SVM 因為資料只有 400 筆、用 RBF 核因為線性分不開、瓶頸在特徵不在分類器。
Project only reaches 78%, but he can explain: chose SVM because there are only 400 data points, used an RBF kernel because the data isn't linearly separable, and the bottleneck is the features, not the classifier.

專案分數上 A 會贏。但這門課想培養的是 B。考試就是用來補這個缺口的。
On project score alone, A wins. But this course wants to cultivate B. The exams exist precisely to fill that gap.
「你的資料線性不可分。請比較『改用軟間隔』與『改用核方法』兩條路的假設、代價與適用時機。」
"Your data is not linearly separable. Compare the assumptions, costs, and appropriate use cases of switching to a soft margin versus switching to a kernel method."
—— 這種題目沒辦法靠拼裝 repo 回答，也沒辦法靠 AI 直接生出來，因為它要的是你的判斷 | A question like this can't be answered by stitching together repos, nor generated directly by AI — because it demands your own judgment

這門課的配分設計，刻意讓你無法只靠單一項目過關。

The weighting is deliberately designed so no single item can carry you through alone. This isn't being difficult for its own sake — pattern recognition, as a field, genuinely requires all three.

知識檢定 | Knowledge Check

1
結構理解 | Understanding the Structure
把六個項目歸成三群，各是多少 %？各驗收什麼能力？
Group the six items into three, what percentage is each, and what ability does each check?
✅ 正確答案：
考試 50%（驗收方法論理解）、期末專案 35%（驗收實作與 AI 協作）、隨堂測驗 15%（驗收學習節奏）。比例上是「理解 > 產出 > 過程」，但三者缺一不可。
Exams 50% (checks methodological understanding), final project 35% (checks implementation and AI collaboration), quizzes 15% (checks learning pace). By proportion it's "understanding > output > process," but none of the three can be skipped.
2
數值計算 | Numerical Calculation
若隨堂平均 90、考試平均 55、專案 75，學期總分是多少？
If the quiz average is 90, exam average 55, and project 75, what's the semester total?
✅ 正確答案：
$15\%\times0.90 = 13.5$；$50\%\times0.55 = 27.5$；$35\%\times0.75 = 26.25$。
合計 67.25 分。注意：考試只要再提高 10 分（55→65），總分就多 5 分——50% 的權重讓考試成為投報率最高的施力點。
$15\%\times0.90=13.5$; $50\%\times0.55=27.5$; $35\%\times0.75=26.25$. Total: 67.25. Note: raising the exam score by just 10 points (55→65) adds 5 points to the total — the 50% weight makes exams the highest-leverage place to invest effort.
3
制度理解 | Understanding the System
為什麼構想書只佔 5%，卻是整個專案設計中最關鍵的一環？
Why is the proposal only 5%, yet the single most critical piece of the whole project design?
✅ 參考答案：
因為它的價值不在分數，而在時間與回饋。第 10 週交構想書，代表方向錯誤會在還有七週可轉圜時被發現；若沒有這道關卡，錯誤方向要到第 16、17 週才會暴露，屆時已無法補救。 5% 的低配分同時確保它不會變成另一個高風險項目，學生才願意誠實寫出還沒想清楚的地方——與 KP-05 隨堂測驗的低風險設計是同一套邏輯。
Its value isn't in the score but in time and feedback. Submitting the proposal in week 10 means a wrong direction gets caught with seven weeks still left to correct it; without this checkpoint, a wrong direction wouldn't surface until weeks 16–17, too late to fix. Its low 5% weight also ensures it doesn't become another high-stakes item, so students are willing to honestly write down what they haven't figured out yet — the same logic as the low-stakes quiz design in KP-05.

總結回顧 | Summary

理解（50%）比產出（35%）重要，但產出不能沒有；
而過程（15%）便宜，卻是前兩者的地基。
Understanding (50%) matters more than output (35%), but output can't be skipped; process (15%) is cheap, yet it's the foundation the other two stand on.

從第 1 週就開始準備期末專案，不是為了專案——是為了讓你在第 17 週能專心考試。
Starting the final project from week 1 isn't for the project's sake — it's so that by week 17 you can focus entirely on the exam.
